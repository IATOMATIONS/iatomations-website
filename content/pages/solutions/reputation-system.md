 ## The Feedback Infrastructure
 We implement a systematic, set-and-forget mechanism that intercepts feedback before it goes public and amplifies praise.


 

 
 
 
 
 ### Automated Triggers
 We connect GoHighLevel to your POS, Stripe, or booking system. The moment a service is marked "completed" or a product arrives, a strategic SMS or Email sequence fires automatically.


 

 
 
 
 ### Smart Routing (Gatekeeping)
 We build an intermediate landing page. If they rate you 4-5 stars, they are instantly routed to your Google Business Profile. If they rate you 1-3 stars, the feedback is sent internally to management, protecting your public image.


 

 
 
 
 ### Centralized Review Dashboard
 View and respond to reviews from Google, Facebook, and Yelp all from a single GoHighLevel dashboard. Use AI to draft professional, personalized responses in seconds to maintain an active profile.
