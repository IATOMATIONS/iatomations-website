 ## The Initial Audit: "A Ferrari on a Dirt Road"
 
 When we performed the forensic analysis on Quntus Aromas, we found a premium, high-quality product trapped in an informal digital infrastructure.


 
 
 
 #### Technical Invisibility & Brand Dilution
 They were operating on an outdated .ws domain, which damaged international trust. Furthermore, lacking a claimed Google Business Profile meant they were losing local traffic to competitors with similar names. Their catalog was completely disconnected from Meta and Google.

 ## The Transformation: The Omnichannel Sync Framework
 We didn't just build a website; we paved the road for their Ferrari engine. We executed a zero-downtime migration to a robust Shopify infrastructure under a secured global domain (quntus-as.com).


 
 
 
 #### 100% Tracking & Automated Recovery
 We deployed Server-Side Tracking via GTM and Meta CAPI. Finally, we integrated GoHighLevel to centralize their inbox and deployed automated cart recovery workflows via WhatsApp and Email, rescuing lost sales while they sleep.
