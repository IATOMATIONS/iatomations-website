 ## The Initial Audit: "High-Ticket Friction"
 
 Engineering projects require deep consultations. When we audited Engco S.A.S, we found a highly capable technical team bogged down by administrative chaos.


 
 
 
 #### Leaky Pipeline & Scheduling Chaos
 High-value B2B leads were falling through the cracks. The process of qualifying a prospect and manually going back and forth via email to schedule an on-site or virtual technical visit was causing severe delays and lost revenue.

 ## The Transformation: The B2B Automation Engine
 We re-engineered their entire acquisition flow. We launched a lightning-fast WordPress architecture natively connected to a centralized CRM (GoHighLevel).


 
 
 
 #### AI Qualification & Smart Scheduling
 We deployed an AI Agent to ask qualifying technical questions to new leads. Once qualified, the system routes them to an Automated Booking Engine where they can schedule virtual consultations or on-site engineering visits, instantly populating the sales pipeline.


 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 ## The Impact
 A flawless sales cycle that allows engineers to focus on engineering, not administration.

 #### Pipeline Visibility
