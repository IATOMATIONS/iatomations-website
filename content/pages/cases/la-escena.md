 ## The Challenge: "Starting with a Blank Canvas"
 
 Launching an event production and artist booking agency requires flawless logistics. La Escena EC was starting from zero and needed a solid digital foundation from day one.


 
 
 
 #### Scheduling Chaos & Lost Leads
 Managing artist availability, event inquiries, and high-ticket bookings manually through emails or DMs is a recipe for double-bookings and missed opportunities. They needed an architecture that could organize the chaos automatically.
